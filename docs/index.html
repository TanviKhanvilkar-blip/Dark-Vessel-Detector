Dark vessel detection

Radar satellites (Sentinel-1) see ships whether or not they announce themselves. AIS is what ships broadcast. This system detects ships in radar images with a CFAR detector, projects every AIS ship to the exact image time, pairs them with the Hungarian algorithm, and lists the ships that radar saw but AIS did not: candidates for review, never accusations.

How to read a flag. "Not publicly tracked" is not "switched off". Small boats often carry no public AIS, free AIS comes from coastal receivers with limited range, and ships under ~40 m are not reliably visible. Low-confidence flags are those the data cannot support (beyond receiver range, AIS feed gap, or no AIS recorded).
